# Local SEO launch plan

## Already implemented

- Static Astro output with indexable HTML.
- Unique title, description, canonical URL and H1 for every page.
- Dedicated pages for residential, commercial, office, move-in/out and deep cleaning.
- Dedicated service-area pages for Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens and the Bronx.
- `LocalBusiness`, `WebSite`, `Service`, `BreadcrumbList` and `FAQPage` structured data.
- Crawlable internal links, `robots.txt` and XML sitemap.
- Responsive layout, reduced-motion support and optimized WebP imagery.

## Required after launch

1. Verify the final domain in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://lulescleaningservice.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. Verify and fully complete the Google Business Profile under the exact public business name.
4. Select the most accurate primary business category and add only real secondary categories.
5. Add the real service areas, opening hours, phone, website and service descriptions.
6. Upload original work photos regularly and request honest customer reviews.
7. Reply to every review professionally.
8. Keep the business name, phone and service details consistent across the website, Google profile and reputable directories.
9. Add the remaining Lules Multiservices categories only after their exact names and scope are confirmed.

No provider can guarantee first place in Google. Local visibility depends on relevance, distance and prominence as well as website quality.
